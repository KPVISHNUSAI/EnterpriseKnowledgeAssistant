# Information Security Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy protects the information and systems of Northwind Aerial Robotics, including customer flight data processed in SkyFleet. It applies to employees, contractors and suppliers with access to company systems. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns the policy.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and SkyFleet administration accounts. Shared accounts are prohibited except for documented break-glass accounts held by the Security team.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after 5 minutes of inactivity. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour, so that it can be wiped remotely. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mail app.

## Data classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted information, such as executive pay, deal plans and customer imagery covered by a non-disclosure agreement, may only be stored in systems approved by the Security team and shared with named individuals.

## Access reviews and exceptions

Access to production systems is reviewed every 90 days by the system owner. Accounts of people who leave the company are disabled on their last working day. Exceptions to this policy must be approved by the CISO and are valid for at most 6 months.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre at security@northwind.example or on internal extension 4400 within 1 hour of discovery. Do not try to investigate an incident yourself.
