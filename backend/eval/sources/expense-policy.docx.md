# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting expenses

Expense reports must be submitted in the Ledgerly app within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted more than 90 days after the expense date are rejected unless the Chief Financial Officer grants an exception. Each report covers one trip or one calendar month.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of USD 25 or more. Card slips without item details are not accepted as receipts. A lost receipt can be replaced by a signed missing-receipt declaration at most twice per year.

## Approval thresholds

Every expense report is approved by one person, chosen by the report total.

| Report total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 500 | Line manager |
| USD 500.01 to USD 5,000 | Department director |
| More than USD 5,000 | VP Finance |

## Non-reimbursable items

The company does not reimburse traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment subscriptions, seat upgrades bought for personal comfort, or travel insurance that duplicates the corporate policy.

## Corporate cards and payment

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times per year. Card statements must be reconciled in Ledgerly by the fifth business day of the following month. Approved out-of-pocket expenses are paid with the next payroll run, usually within 10 business days.
