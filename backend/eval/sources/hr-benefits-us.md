# US Employee Benefits Guide

This guide applies to employees of Northwind Aerial Robotics Inc. who work from the Austin office or remotely in the United States.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year plus 11 company holidays. Up to 40 hours of unused paid time off can be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks at full pay, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. Employees become eligible after 90 days of employment.

## Retirement plan

The company matches 100% of the first 4% of salary that employees contribute to the 401(k) plan. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Health and sick leave

Employees choose between three medical plans, and the company pays 90% of the employee premium. Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
