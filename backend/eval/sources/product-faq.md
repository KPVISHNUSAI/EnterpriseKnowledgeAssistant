# Kestrel X2 Product FAQ

## How long can the Kestrel X2 fly?

Up to 42 minutes with the standard battery and no payload. With the maximum payload of 2.5 kg, flight time drops to about 31 minutes.

## Can the Kestrel X2 fly in bad weather?

The airframe is rated IP54 against dust and water spray. It operates between -10 °C and 45 °C and in sustained winds of up to 12 m/s.

## How long does charging take?

A battery charges from empty to 100% in 70 minutes with the fast charger, or in about 2 hours with the standard charger.

## What warranty is included?

The airframe has a 2-year limited warranty. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 300 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## Can I fly at night?

The Kestrel X2 has anti-collision strobes that are visible from 3 km. Whether night operations are allowed depends on local aviation rules and the operator's permits.

## Which payloads are supported?

The RGB mapping camera, the TX-9 thermal camera and the LS-2 multispectral sensor. Payloads are hot-swappable and are detected automatically.
