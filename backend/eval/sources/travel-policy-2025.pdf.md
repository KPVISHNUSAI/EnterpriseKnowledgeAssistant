# Travel Policy 2025

## Scope and effective dates

This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Northwind Aerial Robotics who travel on company business between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025. It was approved by the Chief Financial Officer on 2 December 2024 and is owned by the Finance team. Questions about the policy go to the Travel Desk.

## Booking and approval

All trips must be booked through the TravelDesk portal at least 14 days before departure. Trips that leave the employee's home country require written approval from a director before booking. Trips booked outside the portal are reimbursed only when the portal could not offer a fare for the same route.

## Air travel

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 10 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 10 hours or longer. Business class is not reimbursed under the 2025 policy, whatever the length of the flight. Employees keep the frequent flyer miles they earn on business trips.

## Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is USD 180 in standard cities and USD 250 in high-cost cities. High-cost cities are London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo and Zurich. Stays longer than 14 nights should use serviced apartments booked by the Travel Desk.

## Meals and ground transport

The daily meal per diem is USD 55 per travel day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Personal cars used for business trips are reimbursed at USD 0.58 per mile. Rental cars need approval from the line manager, and the compact car category is the default.
