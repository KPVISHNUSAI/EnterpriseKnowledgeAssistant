# Travel Policy 2026

## Scope and effective dates

This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Northwind Aerial Robotics who travel on company business from 1 January 2026. It replaces the Travel Policy 2025 and was approved by the Chief Financial Officer on 28 November 2025. The Finance team owns the policy and reviews it every year.

## Booking and approval

All trips must be booked through the TravelDesk portal at least 10 days before departure. Trips that leave the employee's home country require written approval from a director before booking. Rail is the preferred option for journeys that take less than 4 hours door to door.

## Air travel

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 8 hours. Business class may be booked for flights of 8 hours or longer when a vice president approves the trip in advance. The Travel Desk buys carbon offsets for every flight booked through the portal.

## Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is USD 210 in standard cities and USD 290 in high-cost cities. High-cost cities are London, New York, San Francisco, Singapore, Tokyo and Zurich. Stays longer than 14 nights should use serviced apartments booked by the Travel Desk.

## Meals and ground transport

The daily meal per diem is USD 65 per travel day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Personal cars used for business trips are reimbursed at USD 0.62 per mile. Rental cars need approval from the line manager, and electric or hybrid models are preferred where available.
