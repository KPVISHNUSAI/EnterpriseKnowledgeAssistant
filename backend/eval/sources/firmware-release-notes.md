# Kestrel Firmware Release Notes

## Version 4.2.0

Released on 3 June 2026 for the Kestrel X2.

- Return-to-home now triggers automatically at 25% battery.
- Obstacle avoidance range increased to 40 m.
- The geofence altitude ceiling defaults to 120 m and can be raised to 150 m with an operator waiver.
- Known issue: compass calibration can fail below -5 °C; calibrate indoors before cold-weather flights.

## Version 4.1.0

Released on 12 January 2026 for the Kestrel X2.

- Return-to-home triggers automatically at 20% battery.
- Obstacle avoidance range is 30 m.
- Adds support for the TX-9 thermal camera payload.
- Fixes a bug that reset the home point after a battery swap.

## Upgrading

Install updates from the SkyFleet console or with the Kestrel Field app. Drones need at least 50% battery during an update, and a firmware update takes about 12 minutes.
