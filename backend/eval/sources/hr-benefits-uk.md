# UK Employee Benefits Guide

This guide applies to employees of Northwind Aerial Robotics UK Ltd who work from the Bristol office or remotely in the United Kingdom.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over and must be taken by 31 March of the following year.

## Parental leave

Primary carers receive 26 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by 13 weeks at statutory pay. Secondary carers receive 4 weeks at full pay, which can be taken in blocks of one week within the first year. To qualify, employees need 26 weeks of continuous service by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth or placement.

## Pension

The company pays an employer pension contribution of 7% of base salary. Employees contribute at least 3% through salary sacrifice. Contributions start in the first month of employment.

## Health and sick pay

Private medical insurance from Meridale Health (a fictional insurer) covers employees and their dependants from the first day. Sick pay is 20 days at full pay in any rolling 12-month period.
