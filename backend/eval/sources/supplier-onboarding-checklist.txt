Supplier Onboarding Checklist

Before the first purchase order, every new supplier must complete the security questionnaire, provide a certificate of insurance covering at least USD 2 million, and be approved by the Procurement team.

Payment terms for suppliers are net 45 days. Invoices are paid only after Procurement has matched them to an approved purchase order.
