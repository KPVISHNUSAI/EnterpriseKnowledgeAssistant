# Kestrel X2 Field Maintenance Guide

## Propellers

Replace the propellers every 200 flight hours, or immediately when a blade shows a chip, crack or bend. Always replace propellers in complete sets of four.

## Motors

Inspect the motors every 50 flight hours. Spin each motor by hand and replace it if it grinds, runs hot after landing or shows visible play in the bearing.

## Battery storage

Store batteries at 40 to 60% charge in a dry place between 15 °C and 25 °C. Batteries that swell or lose more than 20% of their capacity must be retired.

## Technician notes

Technicians must log every repair in the SkyFleet maintenance log within 24 hours.
