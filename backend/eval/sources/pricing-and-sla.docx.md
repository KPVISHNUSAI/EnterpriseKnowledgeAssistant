# SkyFleet Pricing and Service Level Agreement

## Plans

| Plan | Price per drone per month | Included storage | Support hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | USD 49 | 50 GB | Business hours, Monday to Friday |
| Professional | USD 129 | 500 GB | 24 hours a day, 5 days a week |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing | 5 TB | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week |

Annual prepayment gives a 15% discount on the Starter and Professional plans.

## Uptime commitment and service credits

| Plan | Monthly uptime commitment | Service credit if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 99.5% | None |
| Professional | 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| Enterprise | 99.95% | 25% of the monthly fee |

Service credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Scheduled maintenance announced 5 days in advance does not count as downtime.

## Support response times

| Severity | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| P1 - fleet grounded | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P2 - service degraded | 8 business hours | 4 hours |
| P3 - question | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Starter customers receive support by email with a target response time of 2 business days.
