SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - Northwind Aerial Robotics (fictional): Remote Work Policy. Written for evaluation and demos; it describes no real company.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week when their manager agrees. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's office time zone, and team meetings should be scheduled inside these hours.

A one-time home office stipend of USD 400 is paid after the probation period. Employees of the UK company receive GBP 320 instead. The stipend covers a desk, a chair or a monitor; laptops and phones are provided by IT.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year with approval from the People team. Longer stays need a tax and immigration review before travel.

Flight test engineers and laboratory technicians must work on site because their work needs the test range and lab equipment, so remote work does not apply to these roles.
