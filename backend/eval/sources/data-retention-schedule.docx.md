# Data Retention Schedule

## How to use this schedule

Each record type is kept for the period below and then deleted by automated jobs that run on the first Sunday of each month. A legal hold issued by the Legal team overrides this schedule until the hold is released.

## Retention periods

| Record type | Retention period | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Financial records and invoices | 7 years after the end of the financial year | Finance |
| Employee personnel files | 6 years after employment ends | People team |
| Job applicant records | 12 months after the hiring decision | People team |
| Customer flight telemetry | 24 months | Platform engineering |
| Raw flight imagery | 90 days unless the customer contract says otherwise | Platform engineering |
| Support tickets | 3 years after the ticket is closed | Customer success |
| Security audit logs | 2 years | Security |

## Requests for deletion

Customers can ask for earlier deletion of their flight data through their account manager. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed in writing.
